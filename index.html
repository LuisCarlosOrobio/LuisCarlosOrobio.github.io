Compilers, symbolic engines, and machine learning systems.

I build mathematical substrate: the layer underneath the thing most people build. A compiler that emits GPU kernels rather than a model trained on them. An algebra that derives what a spacetime is from its metric rather than a renderer that draws one.

The method doesn't change with the language. The structure of the problem — 208 tensor operations, the tensor algebra of general relativity, Clifford algebra, the state of a world — goes into an algebraic data type, so the compiler refuses to let a case go unhandled. The core stays pure and total; GPUs, networks, and clocks live at the edges. Then it's checked against something known: analytic gradients, closed-form solutions, exact replay. So far that's been ReScript, OCaml, Julia, and Python, and it has been the same idea every time.

    Andropia

    Python · Three.js · VRM · WebGL

    A deterministic sandbox for embodied intelligence: language models inhabiting bodies in a shared 3D world, perceiving it, moving through it, and talking to each other. Beings are told what is around them in words rather than coordinates, and act by writing tags inline in their speech.

    The world step is a pure function of state, intents, and dt. A model's reply is never a state change — it becomes intents, and the tick that applies them records them. So a seed plus the intent log replays any run exactly, without calling a model, whatever the temperature was and whether or not the endpoint still exists. 533 tests, no browser required.

    Begun in 2023 as a 3D world for locally hosted models to move around in, and rebuilt from scratch in 2026 around the deterministic core. What prompted the rewrite: three beings once spent two minutes discussing the falling water level of a pond that was a point on a flat plane, having agreed on a glowing seam in a rock that didn't have one. Nothing they said contradicted what they'd been told, because they'd been told nothing. The transcript, the world, and the render had come apart.

  • 2026

    visual-web-ai

    ReScript · WGSL · WebGPU

    A neural network compiler and runtime that runs entirely in the browser. ReScript generates optimized WGSL compute shaders, WebGPU executes them on the GPU, autograd handles backpropagation. No server.

    208 operations as algebraic variants, which means adding one breaks the build until shape inference, a forward kernel, and a backward kernel all exist — a missing gradient is a compile error rather than a wrong number at 3am. INT4 GPTQ matmul, tested against Qwen 2.5 7B in a tab.

  • 2025

    GeometricAI.jl

    Julia · Geometric algebra · Riemannian optimization

    A from-scratch geometric machine learning library: Clifford algebra Cl(3,0), SO(3)/SE(3) Lie groups, Riemannian manifolds with curvature, intrinsic manifold learning, Riemannian Adam that parallel-transports momentum, and a neural layer whose forward pass is the geometric product — multivector weights, exact analytic gradients rather than autodiff.

    Registered in Julia's General registry, so it installs with Pkg.add("GeometricAI"). 94 tests against known references, and the README says plainly which parts are verified and which are first-order approximations. Built as the engine for a VR application where you sculpt a classifier by gesturing at the geometry of your data.

  • 2024

    Symbolic tensor calculus for general relativity

    OCaml · js_of_ocaml · Three.js

    Give it a metric tensor and it derives the Christoffel symbols, Riemann and Ricci tensors, Einstein tensor, and geodesics by exact symbolic differentiation. Nothing is hardcoded and every algebraic step is traceable, so it shows the derivation rather than just the answer. Metric-agnostic: nothing downstream of the metric knows what a wormhole is. Compiles to JavaScript and runs in the browser alongside a Three.js embedding diagram.

    Cinegraphs

    Qwen3-VL · Unsloth/LoRA · FastAPI

    A vision pipeline extracts narrative structure from a hundred-plus art films into a 10k-example dataset, used to fine-tune Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct so that it generates scripts by sampling scene graphs rather than continuing a sequence left to right.
